# 3D-wooden-house
A Showa-era wooden house built in the 1940s (or earlier), where a young family of four lived for several years after World War II, beginning their life and career journey in Tainan City, Taiwan.

Built in the Japanese colonial period, this modest Japanese-style wooden house on Beizhong Street, Tainan, sheltered a young family of four for several years as they embarked on their life and career adventures. Nestled between the vibrant pulse of Tainan Railway Station and the sacred local community of Fulong Temple (福隆宮), the residence witnessed decades of daily life, bustling alleyway chatter, and Tainan's urban transformation.
This modest Japanese-style wooden row house—known as Nagaya (長屋)—dates back to the Japanese colonial period-the Showa ear, most likely built in the 1930s–1940s. Characterized by its cedar timber framing, horizontal rainscreen weatherboards, and dark Japanese roof tiles, it embodies a classic pre-war urban dwelling.

This 3D digital heritage preservation project created by Elements Lab (實驗元素工作室), reconstructing a Showa-era wooden house in Tainan, Taiwan, and reviving the everyday memories of the 1960s.
